# Rake V - How to Use 🎵

## 🗂 Importing Playlists
- Click the **“+” / Import Folder** button.  
- Select your main music folder.  
- Each subfolder becomes a separate playlist.  
- Example: `Music/Rap`, `Music/Chill`, `Music/Mixes`.  
- Playlists appear in the left panel. Click a playlist to view its songs.

## 🎶 Playing Music
- Click a song to start playing immediately.  
- Album artwork and vinyl rotation are displayed in the center.  
- Vinyl animation slows or stops when music is paused.

### Playback Controls & Keybinds
- **Play / Pause:** Click ▶/II or press **Space**  
- **Next Song:** Click ▶| or press **Ctrl + Right**  
- **Previous Song:** Click |◀ or press **Ctrl + Left**  
- **Shuffle:** Click 🔀 or press **Ctrl + S**  
- **Repeat Song:** Click 🔁 or press **Ctrl + R**  
- **Mute / Unmute Volume:** Click 🔉 or press **Ctrl + M**  
- **Seek:** Drag the progress bar under the album artwork  
- **Search:** Click the search bar or press **Ctrl + F**

## 🔍 Search & YouTube Downloads
- Type in the search bar to search across all playlists.  
- Press **Enter** or click a result to play it immediately.  
- If no results are found, an option to download from YouTube appears.  
- Downloaded songs are automatically saved into the selected playlist.

## 💾 Auto-Save & Resume
Rake V automatically remembers your playback state, including:  
- Last played song  
- Selected playlist  
- Queue order  
- Volume level  
- Repeat / Shuffle state  
- Playback resumes where you left off.

## 🎨 Customization & Tips
- Drag the top bar to move the application window.  
- Buttons provide subtle click animations for visual feedback.  
- Keep playlist folder names simple for optimal results.  
- Playlist folders should only contain **.mp3** files.
